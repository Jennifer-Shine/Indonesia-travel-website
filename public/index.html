My Travel Story & Journey

I departed from Cambodia and flew to Singapore before taking another flight to Bali, Indonesia, to begin the trip I had been looking forward to for a long time.

My first destination was the famous Kuta Beach. I enjoyed the blue ocean, the soft sandy shore, and the breathtaking sunset over the sea. Later, I traveled inland to Ubud, where I explored the world-famous rice terraces surrounded by lush tropical forests. I also tried the exciting jungle swing, which offered a panoramic view of the valley, and visited ancient Balinese temples decorated with beautiful stone carvings.

Besides visiting scenic attractions, I also experienced the local lifestyle. I strolled through the city streets at night, tasted a variety of fresh seafood, Western-style steaks, and traditional Indonesian dishes. I relaxed at a resort with a private swimming pool and enjoyed cold drinks from Starbucks while exploring the city.

The warm hospitality of the local people, the unique Southeast Asian culture, and the stunning natural landscapes made this trip truly special and unforgettable. It was a valuable experience that I will always cherish.

Top Destinations I Visited

Kuta Beach, Bali

Iconic coastal landmark of Bali, crystal clear turquoise seawater, soft sand beach with beach umbrellas and surf lovers. I spent whole afternoon enjoying sea breeze, watched gorgeous gradient sunset dyed sky orange and purple after dusk, and took night shot at Kuta Beach landmark signboard.

Ubud Tropical Rice Terraces

Nestled in Ubud’s rainforest valley, tiered green rice fields surrounded by towering coconut palm trees. I experienced famous jungle swing hanging above valley, overlooking rolling terraced landscape, and visited antique Balinese stone temple with delicate traditional sculpture.

Local Food & Resort Leisure

Tried luxurious seafood platter with fresh lobster & oyster, western grilled steak and creamy mushroom pasta. I also stayed at tropical garden resort with private pool surrounded by dense green plants and palm trees, enjoyed slow relaxing holiday vibe.
